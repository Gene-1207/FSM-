Junction Keeper

You choose which vehicles enter the junction and from which road. A signal controller (a finite state machine) decides who goes, so nothing collides, and it clears the way for ambulances. Traffic keeps left.

    How the controller decides
    • Green lasts at least 5 s and at most 14 s, and only ends when the other road has vehicles waiting.
    • Yellow lasts 1.4 s. All red follows and waits until the junction box is empty, so nobody is inside when the crossing road starts.
    • Ambulance on the road that has green: green is held until it clears. On the crossing road: green ends early, the junction clears, then it gets green.
    • Vehicles brake for red lights and for the vehicle ahead. One too close to stop when yellow starts finishes crossing, and all red waits for it.
